Step 0 · 5 minutes, shared with the brief

Which painpoint is yours?

Pick the card the group feels most strongly. If none of them is yours, write your own along the bottom.

Step 1 · still inside the 5 minutes

Write the idea brief.

  • 1Name your agent. Short, like EDD guru.
  • 2What is the agent doing for you?
  • 3What do you expect to see and receive?
  • 4What changes for you, or for the client?

Step 2 · 10 minutes

Design three screens.

Imagine you are directing the story. Write one or two sentences for each step, then press Generate screen. The AI assistant only helps with the screen you have open.

Step 3 · 15 minutes

Build the prototype.

Generate it, then make one or two changes. That is enough. When the three screens are done, go back to Screen 1.
